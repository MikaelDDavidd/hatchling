Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Hatchling is open source under the MIT license, and the Mac app updates itself when a new version comes out.

Which agents does it work with?

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, Copilot, Qoder, Factory, CodeBuddy, OpenCode, Trae, Qwen Code, Droid and a few more. On first launch it adds its hooks to every CLI it finds.

Does my code leave the Mac?

No. Hatchling reads your sessions locally. The phone link is optional: when you turn it on, session state goes through a relay to your paired phones, and nothing listens for incoming connections on your Mac.

How do I get the iPhone app?

It is in private beta. Open an issue on GitHub and ask for an invite.

What does Hatch learn about me?

Things you mention while you work, like your stack or your team, so it has something to talk about. It all stays in one file on your Mac that you can read, edit or wipe in Settings.
